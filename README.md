Medicine Management System
1. Project Title
Medicine Management System
2. Project Description
The Medicine Management System is designed to manage medicines, suppliers, stock, sales, and quantities in an organized way. It helps maintain medicine records, track available stock, record sales, and manage supplier information efficiently.
3. Objectives
Store medicine details.
Manage supplier information.
Track medicine stock and quantity.
Record medicine sales.
Maintain accurate and organized records.
Reduce manual work and errors.
4. Main Entities
Medicine – Stores details of medicines.
Supplier – Stores supplier information.
Stock – Maintains available medicine stock.
Sale – Stores sales/transaction details.
Quantity – Represents the quantity of medicines involved in stock and sales.
5. Basic Algorithm
Start the system.
Enter or manage medicine details.
Add and manage supplier details.
Add medicine stock and quantity.
Record medicine sales.
Update stock quantity after each sale.
Display or manage the required records.
Stop the system.
6. ER Diagram
The ER diagram represents the relationships between Medicine, Supplier, Stock, Sale, and Quantity, including their primary keys, foreign keys, and relationships/cardinalities.
7. Key Features
Medicine management
Supplier management
Stock management
Quantity tracking
Sales management
Record maintenance
8. Conclusion
